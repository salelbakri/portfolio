Background & Vision

Bridging Physical Engineering with Modern AI

Where domain precision in physical systems meets probabilistic reasoning and large-scale data analytics.

My journey began in Electrical & Systems Engineering, where I worked with regional power grids and pharmaceutical manufacturing systems. At ISO New England, I maintained Energy Management System (EMS) models representing bulk electrical transmission and generation across six states—where model accuracy is essential for grid stability.

The Pivot to Data Science: Power grids are the most complex machines mankind has built. As energy transitions to renewables and distributed nodes, raw physical equations must be augmented with data-driven predictive models, automated agents, and intelligent workflows.

Now pursuing my Master of Science in Data Science at the University of Washington, I apply modern machine learning, LLM autonomous agent systems, and computer vision to solve data-rich challenges. Whether fine-tuning neural networks for clinical MRI classification, orchestrating multi-turn LLM tools for Flipkart, or optimizing SQL analytics at Bonneville Power Administration, I bring an engineer’s rigor to data science.
